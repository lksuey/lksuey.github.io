The collection sets the pace

The page brings together collections, the store introduction, service stages, frequently asked questions, and contact. Prata headings, Karla body text, a light background, and deep red accents give the photographs room to lead.

Each dress has a contextual WhatsApp link with the item already identified. Service steps and FAQs help visitors prepare for the conversation and a potential fitting.

From the dress to the conversation

The collection uses photographs from the original project materials. This is a collection presentation with an inquiry workflow, rather than an e-commerce checkout or stock system.

The mobile layout preserves collection browsing and contextual contact. The delivered improvement is a clearer route to a fitting inquiry; the prospecting concept has no measured sales or conversion results.
